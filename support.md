# 🎄 Elfventory — Support & FAQ

Welcome to Elfventory Support!

Elfventory helps you organize Christmas gifts, manage recipients, track holiday spending, and keep your shopping plans in one place.

Available for iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Elfventory?

Elfventory is a Christmas gift planning and tracking app. It helps you organize gift ideas, keep track of purchases, manage budgets, and plan Christmas for the people on your list.

### Can I use Elfventory on multiple devices?

Yes. Elfventory supports iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with iCloud synchronization across compatible devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

### Where is my gift information stored?

Elfventory uses Apple's private iCloud storage to synchronize your gift information. See our [Privacy Policy](privacy.html) for additional details.

### Can I organize gifts for different Christmas years?

Yes. Elfventory lets you organize your Christmas planning by year.

### Can I add photos and shopping links?

Yes. Elfventory supports photos, product information, and shopping links to help you keep track of potential gifts and purchases.

### Can I protect my gift lists from curious family members?

Elfventory offers optional app-lock protection to help keep your Christmas surprises private.

### What should I do if my information isn't syncing?

1. Confirm your devices are signed in to the same Apple Account.
2. Check that iCloud is enabled and your internet connection is working.
3. Open Elfventory on each device to allow synchronization.
4. If the problem continues, contact support.

### How do I report a problem or suggest a feature?

We welcome questions, feedback, and suggestions.

Email us with a description of the issue, your device type, and any relevant details.
