# Elfventory Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: November 13, 2026**

## Overview

Elfventory is a Christmas gift planning and tracking application for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. We respect your privacy and believe your personal gift information should remain under your control.

## Information You Enter

Elfventory allows you to save information such as gift ideas, recipient names, shopping lists, budgets, purchase details, notes, photos, receipts, and product links.

This information is used to provide the app's features.

## Data Storage and iCloud

Elfventory uses Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services to store and synchronize your information across your compatible devices.

Your gift information is associated with your Apple Account and stored using Apple's private iCloud infrastructure.

Elfventory does not require a separate developer-hosted account.

Apple's handling of iCloud information is governed by Apple's privacy practices.

## Photos and Shared Content

Elfventory may access photos or information shared from other apps when you choose to use those features.

The information you select is used to create or update your gift records.

## App Security

Elfventory offers optional app-lock protection, including a numeric PIN and supported device authentication features.

These features help prevent casual unauthorized access to your gift information.

## Advertising and Data Sales

Elfventory does not sell your personal gift information to advertisers or data brokers.

## Your Control Over Your Information

You control the gift information you add, edit, and delete within Elfventory.

Information synchronized through iCloud may remain in your Apple Account until removed from the applicable iCloud storage.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Elfventory's features change. Updates will be published on this page.
